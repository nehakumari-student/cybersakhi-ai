CyberSakhi AI

An AI-Powered Online Harassment Detection and Digital Safety Guidance Platform

 About the Project

CyberSakhi AI is a proposed AI-powered digital safety platform designed to help women and students understand potentially harmful online communication and access simple digital-safety guidance.

 Problem Statement

Women and students may encounter online harassment, cyberbullying, abusive messages and threatening communication. They may find it difficult to recognise warning signs or understand the appropriate safety steps.

CyberSakhi AI aims to make basic digital-safety guidance easier to access through an AI-powered website.

 Main Features

1. AI Message Analysis: Analyse pasted messages for possible signs of online harassment.
2. Screenshot Text Extraction: Use OCR to extract readable text from uploaded screenshots.
3. AI Digital Safety Chatbot: Provide general guidance about online safety and privacy.
4. Hindi and English Support: Aim to support users in both languages.
5. Trusted Resources: Provide links to official cybercrime-reporting and safety resources.

## Target Users

- Women and girls.
- Students.
- People seeking basic online-safety guidance.

Proposed Development Approach

The project will be developed using AI-powered website-building tools and no-code integrations, without manual programming.

 Project Status

Current stage: Planning and initial repository setup.

Website development, AI integration, OCR integration and chatbot implementation are pending.

 Responsible AI and Privacy

- Use fictional or anonymised examples for testing.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Do not request passwords or private account credentials.
- Clearly explain that AI results may be incorrect.
- Do not treat AI assessments as definitive legal judgements.
